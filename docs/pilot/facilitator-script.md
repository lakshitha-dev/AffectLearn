# Facilitator script

Say the same words to every participant. Text in *italics* is an instruction to the facilitator,
not something to read out.

## Welcome (step 1)

> Thank you for coming. Today you'll use a learning platform called AffectLearn for about forty
> minutes. Before and after, there are some short questions and two short knowledge tests.
> Altogether it takes about seventy minutes.
>
> You can stop at any point, for any reason, just by telling me. It won't affect anything, and
> you don't have to explain why.
>
> Did you get a chance to read the information sheet? Do you have any questions about it?

*Answer questions. Do not describe the two arms or the adaptive behaviour beyond the information
sheet.*

## Consent (step 2)

> Here is the consent form. Please read each statement and initial the ones you agree with.
> Statement 7 is optional. You can take part without it.

*Assign the next participant code, and write the name and code on the linking sheet. Keep the
linking sheet away from the laptop.*

## Setting up (steps 3–5)

*Start the sitting through the admin API. Sign the participant in, typing the prepared
credentials yourself. Turn the laptop towards them.*

> You'll see a consent page on screen. It says the same as the paper form. Please tick the same
> choices you made on paper. Next it asks about the camera. That's up to you. The platform works
> either way.

*If the camera is allowed:*

> This short step just checks that the camera can see your face. Please sit as you normally would.

## Before the course (steps 6–7)

> There's a short questionnaire, and then a quick test on the topic. Don't worry if you don't know
> the answers. That's expected before the course.
>
> The first section is a short warm-up.

## The course (step 8)

> Now please work through the course at your own pace, as you normally would. Every couple of
> sections, a small bar will ask how you're feeling. Answer it or skip it, whichever you like. If
> anything appears on screen, respond to it however you like. I'll be sitting over there. I won't
> talk during the course, so please don't feel you need to talk to me either. If you want to stop,
> just say so.

*Sit out of the participant's line of sight. Do not talk, help or react. Note the start time. At
`[35–40]` minutes, or when the course is finished:*

> Thank you. Let's move on to the last part.

## After the course (steps 9–10)

> There's one more short test, and then three short questionnaires about what it was like to use
> the platform. There are no right or wrong answers.

## Debrief (step 11)

> Thank you, that's everything. Let me explain what the study is about. The platform tries to
> notice from the camera, and from how you use the mouse and keyboard, when someone seems
> disengaged or stuck. In one version of the platform it then responds, for example with a hint or
> a harder question. In the other version it only records. You were in the **[control / adaptive]**
> version. Some sections were deliberately written to feel slow or confusing, so if parts felt
> like that, it was by design. It was not a reflection on you. The platform's readings are a
> computer's guesses, and they are often wrong.
>
> I have a few short questions, if that's all right. I'll take notes but I won't record anything.
>
> 1. Did you notice the platform changing or responding to you while you worked? When?
> 2. *(adaptive)* Was anything it did helpful? Was anything distracting or annoying?
> 3. How did having the camera on feel? *(If it was on.)*
> 4. Was there anything confusing about using the platform itself?
> 5. Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?
>
> If you change your mind about your data, you can ask for it to be deleted until
> **[WITHDRAWAL DEADLINE]**. The contact details are on the information sheet. Do you have any
> questions?

## Close (step 12)

*End the sitting with the reason and any deviation notes. Log out, close the Chrome Guest window,
and check that the camera light is off.*
