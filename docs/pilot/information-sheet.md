## What is the study about?

We are testing a learning platform, AffectLearn. It tries to notice when a learner seems
disengaged or stuck, and it may respond, for example with a hint or a suggestion. We want to learn:
- how well it does this with real learners;
- how people experience it.

## What will I do?

One session of about 70 minutes, in person, on the researcher's laptop:

1. You read and sign a consent form.
2. You answer a short questionnaire and a short knowledge test.
3. You work through a short online course on AI agents for about 35–40 minutes. Some parts may
   feel clear and engaging. Others may feel slow, dense or confusing. That range is part of what
   the study looks at. It is not a test of you.
4. Every couple of sections, you are asked how you feel. You can skip this.
5. After each lesson, you answer four short questions.
6. At the end, you take a second short knowledge test and answer three short questionnaires about
   the experience.
7. The researcher explains the study and asks you a few questions. They take notes only; nothing
   is recorded.

Participants are placed in one of two versions of the platform by chance. You will be told which
at the end.

## What is recorded, and why?

| What | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Webcam measurements**, only if you allow the camera | Images are analysed on the laptop itself. **No photos or video are ever sent or stored.** Once a second, a few numbers are taken from the image and sent: where your eyes point, how open your eyes and mouth are, your head angle, how much you move. | To estimate whether you seem engaged. This is a computer's guess and is often wrong. |
| **Mouse, scroll and keyboard activity** | Pointer position about ten times a second while it moves, clicks and what was clicked, scrolling, whether the tab is visible, and the *kind* of key pressed (letter, digit, space, backspace…) with its timing. **Never which key, never what you type, never passwords.** | To estimate whether you seem confused. |
| **Detailed activity record** (optional, only if you tick it) | The detailed pointer, click, scroll and key-kind record itself, not only the 30-second summaries | So researchers can improve the confusion estimate later |
| **Your answers and progress** | Quiz and test answers, which sections you open and for how long, the help you are offered and what you do with it | To see how learning and help relate |
| **What you tell us** | Your feeling check-ins and questionnaire answers | To compare the computer's guesses with your own view, and to learn about your experience |

**What is not recorded:**
- video or photos;
- audio;
- what you type;
- your name. It appears only on the paper consent form.

**Outside services:**
- Some participants receive help messages written by an AI model. To write them, the text of the
  section you are on, a description of your estimated state (for example "seems disengaged") and
  your recent activity in that section are sent to **OpenAI**. Your name, e-mail and account are
  never sent.
- Video suggestions come from **YouTube**.
- The face-analysis software is downloaded from **Google** and **jsDelivr** servers.
- None of these receives camera images.

## Who will see my data?

- Your records are **pseudonymised**: they carry a study code (for example P007), not your name.
- The researcher keeps the link between your name and your code on paper, separately from the
  data, and destroys it after the withdrawal deadline.
- Only the researcher and the supervisor can access the data.
- Results are reported only as group summaries, never in a form that identifies you.

## Can I stop, or withdraw?

Yes, at any time, without giving a reason and without any consequence:

- **During the session:** tell the researcher, or use "Stop taking part" on your profile page. All
  recording stops straight away. You can also switch the camera off at any time.
- **Deleting your data:** you can ask for everything recorded about you to be deleted, until
  `[WITHDRAWAL DEADLINE]`. After that, the link between your name and your code is destroyed, so
  your data can no longer be identified as yours.
