AffectLearn — Client-Side (Participant) Flows

How a participant moves through the app and how their data is captured, on their device and on the way to the server.

AffectLearn is an affect-aware adaptive learning platform. While a participant studies, the app quietly senses their emotional state (from interaction and, optionally, the webcam) and periodically asks how they feel. This page describes the client side end to end.

1. The participant journey

1
Open AffectLearn → Sign up / Log in. The participant lands on the site and creates an account (or signs in).
2
Consent. They read and agree to take part — no capture happens before consent.
3
Webcam choice. They choose Allow camera (facial + behavioral) or Behavioral-only (camera off — the app still works fully).
4
Calibration (only if the camera is on) — a short step so facial capture is positioned correctly.
5
Pre-study questionnaire. Baseline questions about the learner.
6
Browse → Enroll → Open a lesson. They pick a course and start learning.
7
Study the lesson — the core experience (detailed in section 2).
8
Post-study satisfaction survey, then a Thank-you screen.

2. Inside a lesson — what they see & do

The lesson is a loop. The participant reads one section at a time and marks it complete to move on. Two things appear inline within the page (never as blocking pop-ups):

Self-report check-in (the ground-truth label)

At a natural pause point — after every 3 sections — a small inline bar slides in:

“Quick check: How are you feeling about this material?”

Engaged Confused Bored Frustrated Neutral · or Skip

  • One click, no typing, under 5 seconds. No countdown, no pressure.
  • After a choice it highlights, auto-collapses after ~1s, and shows a quiet “Thanks”.
  • It is an inline bar, not a pop-up / modal / toast — the design rule is “never block learning”.
  • Neutral is a self-report-only option (the AI model uses only the other four).
  • A deliberate Skip is recorded as a skip; a prompt the learner never reaches records nothing.

This is the study’s primary ground-truth label — what the learner says they feel, compared against what the models predicted.

Adaptation cards (adaptive group, Phase B only)

If the participant is in the adaptive group during Phase B, the agent may deliver a gentle inline card based on the detected state:

  • Hint — a simpler explanation when they seem confused.
  • “Skip ahead?” — when the material seems too easy / they look bored.
  • “Take a 5-min break?” — when frustration persists.

The control group sees none of these (same content, no intervention) — that’s the A/B comparison.

3. What the browser captures (mostly invisible)

While the participant simply reads, the browser bundles signals into a ~30-second window and streams it to the server over a single WebSocket. The participant does nothing extra for this.

StreamWhat is capturedVisible to participant?
Behavioralmouse moves & clicks, scrolling, keystroke category (e.g. alphanumeric / backspace — never the content), idle / tab-visibility, timingNo — silent background
Facial (optional)webcam frames, read in memory only and discardedA small “camera on” indicator; no video shown/stored
Self-reportthe one-click feeling at pause pointsYes — the inline “Quick check” bar

4. How the data reaches the server

  1. The browser sends each 30-second behavioral_window (and facial_features if the camera is on, plus self_report at pauses) over the WebSocket.
  2. The backend agent loop runs the Behavioral Bi-LSTM and Facial CNN-LSTM, fuses them, updates the learner profile, and (Phase B) may return an adaptation.
  3. Every classification / interaction / self-report is emitted as a research event → buffered in Redis → drained by a background worker → stored durably in PostgreSQL (research_events).

Onboarding data (consent, webcam choice, questionnaire, survey, A/B group, phase) is written directly to PostgreSQL.

5. What gets stored

Each row in research_events is one of these event types:

  • behavioral_affect_detected · facial_affect_detected · multimodal_affect_detected
  • self_report (ground truth) · adaptation_interaction · learner_profile_updated

And every row carries:

FieldMeaning
affect_state + probabilitiespredicted label + probability over {engaged, bored, confused, frustrated}
confidence, detection_modemodel confidence; behavioral_only / facial / multimodal
learner_id, session_id, cycle_numberwho, which session, which 30-s cycle
timestamp, sequence_numberwhen; monotonic per session (lets analysts detect dropped events)
phase, groupstudy phase (A/B) and cohort (control / adaptive)

6. Privacy

  • No video is ever stored — webcam frames are processed in memory and discarded.
  • Keystroke content is never captured — only the category of a keypress.
  • Consent is required before any capture begins, and the participant can choose behavioral-only (camera off).
